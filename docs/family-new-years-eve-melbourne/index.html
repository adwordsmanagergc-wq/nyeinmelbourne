Family-friendly New Year's Eve in Melbourne 2026

The 9:30pm fireworks are designed for kids, so you can see a full show and be home before midnight. The free Celebration Zones are alcohol-free with entertainment, food trucks and accessible toilets, which makes a big difference with little ones.

Booking tips

  • Aim for the 9:30pm fireworks with younger kids.
  • The Celebration Zones are alcohol-free and family-focused.
  • Pack hats, sunscreen, water, a picnic rug and ear protection for little ones.
  • Agree on a meeting point in case anyone gets separated.
