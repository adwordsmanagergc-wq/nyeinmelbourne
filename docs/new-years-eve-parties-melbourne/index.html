From rooftops in the CBD to bars on the Yarra and a full festival at the Sidney Myer Music Bowl, these are the best NYE parties in Melbourne. Because the midnight fireworks launch from rooftops all over the city, a high terrace or a riverside bar can give you a great view.

Booking tips

  • Many parties release tickets in tiers, so early bird is cheapest.
  • Check whether drinks are all-inclusive or pay as you go.
  • Most parties are 18+ with ID checks.
  • Look for venues that promise fireworks views if the view matters to you.
