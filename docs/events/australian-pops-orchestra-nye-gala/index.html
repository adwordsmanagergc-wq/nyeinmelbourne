Australian Pops Orchestra NYE Gala

A long-running NYE gala concert at Hamer Hall, with a matinee that suits families and an evening show for adults.

What's included

  • John Foreman's 60-piece Australian Pops Orchestra
  • Guest musical-theatre stars
  • Matinee option for families

About Australian Pops Orchestra NYE Gala

Australian Pops Orchestra NYE Gala is at Hamer Hall, Arts Centre Melbourne, 100 St Kilda Rd in Southbank (Kings Domain & Arts Precinct). It's one of the family-friendly options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Melbourne. Fireworks: No view (indoor concert). Age: All ages.
