City of Melbourne Celebration Zones

The official free zones run by the City of Melbourne: entertainment from 6pm, fireworks at 9:30pm and midnight, and no alcohol.

What's included

  • 9:30pm family fireworks and midnight fireworks
  • DJs, silent discos and dance workshops
  • Food trucks (value deals from $15 in 2025)
  • Accessible toilets
  • Alcohol-free (fines apply)

About City of Melbourne Celebration Zones

City of Melbourne Celebration Zones is at Docklands, Kings Domain, Treasury Gardens & Flagstaff Gardens in Melbourne (Melbourne CBD). It's one of the free, family-friendly, vantage points options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Melbourne. Fireworks: Both 9:30pm & midnight. Age: All ages.

Planning the rest of your night? Check the fireworks timeline and transport tips or compare fireworks vantage points around Melbourne.
