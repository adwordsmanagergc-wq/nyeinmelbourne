New Year's Eve in Footscray Park

Maribyrnong Council's free family night by the river, with its own 9:30pm fireworks and an early finish.

What's included

  • 9:30pm fireworks
  • Food trucks and family entertainment
  • Lost-kids wristbands
  • Alcohol-free and pet-free
  • Free shuttles from Footscray Station (2025)

About New Year's Eve in Footscray Park

New Year's Eve in Footscray Park is at Footscray Park, Maribyrnong Blvd in Footscray (Inner West). It's one of the free, family-friendly, vantage points options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Melbourne. Fireworks: 9:30pm. Age: All ages.
