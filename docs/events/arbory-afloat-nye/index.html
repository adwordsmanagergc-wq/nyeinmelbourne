NYE at Arbory Afloat

A floating bar moored on the Yarra beside Flinders Street Station, turned into an all-inclusive river party for the night.

What's included

  • Flowing drinks and canapés from 8pm to midnight (2025)
  • Pop-up food stations
  • DJs and roaming performers
  • Main Deck, VIP Upper Deck and Beach Club packages
  • Views along the Yarra
