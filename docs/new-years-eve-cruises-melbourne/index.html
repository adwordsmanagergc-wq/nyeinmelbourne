New Year's Eve cruises in Melbourne 2026

Being on the water means a front-row seat without the crowds on the banks. Yarra River cruises board at Southbank Promenade or Federation Wharf, while Docklands boats can head out towards Williamstown and the bay. Most are timed for the midnight fireworks, and one family cruise lines up with the 9:30pm show.

Booking tips

  • Check the boarding point and check-in time: boats leave on time.
  • Plan your trip home, as bridges and streets near the river close.
  • Most party and midnight cruises are 18+. Check before booking for families.
  • Dinner cruises sell out early, so book well ahead.
