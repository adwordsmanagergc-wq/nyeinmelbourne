New Year's Eve dinners in Melbourne 2026

A riverside table is the most comfortable way to see in the new year: no crowds to fight, and a long dinner while you wait for midnight. Early sittings are cheaper and often family-friendly, while late sittings take you through to the midnight fireworks.

Booking tips

  • Book early. Riverside tables on Southbank and South Wharf go first.
  • Early sittings are the best-value option and many welcome kids.
  • Ask whether your table has a view of the fireworks or only the venue does.
  • Allow extra travel time: streets and Yarra bridges close during the evening.
